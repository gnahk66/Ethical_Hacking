## Learning outcome
Permission and technical capability are separate requirements. A reachable service or publicly available tool does not authorise testing. A professional engagement needs agreed assets, permitted methods, a testing window, reporting arrangements and stop conditions.

## Applying this learning
Before another lab, record the target machines and network boundaries, take snapshots, agree what disruption is acceptable and define how results will be retained. Preserve evidence rather than treating concealment or log deletion as a professional testing objective.
